Strands Decider 2B · tool selection

It decides, looks, then decides again

Ask a question about a road photograph and most agents do one of two things: run every tool they have, or guess which one to run from the wording of the question. RoadSense does neither. It picks one action, waits to see what comes back, and then picks again — and the second choice is frequently not the one it would have made first. A 2-billion-parameter model called Strands Decider 2B makes every one of those choices, in about 94 ms, without writing a single word.

recorded runs · A10G GPU · nothing staged
1 · the menu

Four actions. Decider scores all of them at once and the bars show the probability it gave each.

2 · withdrawn

An action that is illegal right now — a tool that already ran, OCR with no image — is removed before the model ever sees it.

3 · the evidence

The chosen tool runs and reports back, carrying its own confidence rather than one the agent invented.

4 · the answer

Amazon Bedrock writes it, and never sees the photo — only what the tools found. It cannot describe what was not reported.

and then it loops. The new evidence goes back into the state, the menu gets shorter, and step 1 happens again — until the model picks an action that ends the run, or the step budget does it for them.
