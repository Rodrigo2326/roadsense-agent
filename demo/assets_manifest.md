# Demo asset manifest

Every image in `demo/images/` is **original work**, generated by
[`scripts/make_assets.py`](../scripts/make_assets.py) and licensed Apache-2.0 under
the same terms as the rest of this repository. No photograph, stock image or
third-party asset is used, and nothing was captured while driving.

Synthetic rather than photographed for three reasons:

1. **The licence is unambiguous.** We drew them.
2. **No people, faces or number plates.** Nothing to anonymise, nothing to leak.
3. **Degradation is a dial, not an accident.** The hard cases are reproducible
   because the blur and the downscale are parameters, and they were chosen by
   measuring against the live Rekognition API rather than by eye.

Regenerate them at any time with `python scripts/make_assets.py` — the output is
deterministic.

## How the hard cases were calibrated

A sweep against the live `DetectText` API, reducing the effective width of
`synthetic_sign_clear.jpg`:

| effective width | OCR result | max confidence |
|---|---|---|
| 95 px | `['STOP', 'AHEAD']` | 0.986 |
| 88 px | `['STOP']` | 0.985 ← **partial** |
| 72 px | `['STOP', 'I']` | 0.973 |
| 65 px | `['I']` | 0.854 ← **garbled** |
| 45 px | `[]` | — ← **illegible** |

What the sweep did **not** find is worth recording: with high-contrast block text
there is no level that produces a genuinely low-confidence *correct* reading.
Rekognition's confidence here is bimodal — it reads the word at ~0.98, reads
something else at ~0.85, or returns nothing. A confidence threshold alone therefore
cannot separate right from wrong on this font, and the `garbled` case clears any sane
floor while being wrong. The benchmark reports that rather than tuning a constant
until the number looks good.

## A limitation of the drawn vehicles

Rekognition does not recognise the crude car silhouettes as vehicles. It returns a
`Vehicle` category at 0.60 with **zero instances**. The benchmark cases that ask
"are there any vehicles?" are therefore scored as *insufficient* — not because the
agent failed, but because that is what the evidence honestly supports. A real
photograph would behave differently; this is a property of the asset set and is
stated rather than hidden.

## Safety and scope

- No images were captured while driving. None were captured at all.
- No faces, people, number plates or private property appear.
- The agent's IAM policy permits only `rekognition:DetectLabels` and
  `rekognition:DetectText`. Face and plate recognition are not reachable.
